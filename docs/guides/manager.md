# Finvasia Elevate: guide for Managers

You mark your team's month in **Team**. Everything else (Home, Performance, Reports) shows the results.

## Each month

1. **Open Team.** The month is already open; your people's KPIs and targets have carried over from last month, due on the last day of the month.
2. **Open a person** and work down the page:
   - **1. KPIs:** type the achieved value for each KPI. The **Result** fills in by itself: *Under achieved* below 100% of target, *Achieved* at exactly 100%, *Over achieved* above it.
     For each **duty task**, choose *Completed* or *Not completed* and the date it was completed, and add a delay reason when something is late. On-time and delays come from duty tasks only.
   - **Duty tasks:** the same, without numbers.
   - **2. Ratings:** Tasks on-time, Tasks delayed and KPI average fill in by themselves. Give **Quality** and **CSAT** (1–5). Training fills in from the modules, or type the training score.
   - **3. Training:** record completion dates for the person's modules. To give one module to several people, use **Assign training** on the Team page.
3. **Save**, check **Before you submit**, then **Submit for approval**. Open items can be submitted; missing ratings cannot.
4. If the HOD **returns** a check-in, it shows in Team under **Returned** with the reason. Fix it and submit again.

## Faster ways

- **Excel (mark the whole team in one file):** on the Team page, **Download check-in template** gives one workbook with what is already entered:
  - **Check-ins:** one row per person: Quality and CSAT (1–5, from a dropdown), Training score (TWCS 0–100, optional) and Comments.
  - **KPIs:** type **Achieved**. *% of target* and *Result* (Under achieved / Achieved / Over achieved) fill in by themselves. KPIs have no completion date or delay.
  - **Duty tasks:** **Done?** (Completed / Not completed), **Completed on** and **Delay reason**.
  - **Training:** **Completed on** for each module due this month.
  - **How to use:** the rules in short.

  Edit only the tinted cells. Grey rows are read-only: the check-in is submitted or approved, the month is closed, or someone else marks that person.
  Blank cells change nothing (clear a value on the person's page). Dates can't be in the future; type them like 09 Oct 2026.
  Then **Upload marked template** and **Check file**: the preview lists every change as old → new and every problem, per sheet and row, and saves nothing.
  **Apply** saves the ready rows with the same rules as the person page; rows with problems are skipped. Every change is in the person's history with the file name, sheet and row.
  If someone changed a row in Finvasia Elevate after you downloaded the file, that row is refused: download a fresh file.
- **Filters** on the Team page: To mark, Returned, Submitted, Approved.

## Good to know

- A KPI not marked yet counts as 0 in the KPI average.
- **Edit** on a KPI changes its name, type, target or due date for this month. **Remove** takes it out of this month (with a reason) and stops it carrying over.
- Warnings on a KPI ("to check") can be **marked checked** with a note. Name matches are confirmed by an Admin.
- How the parameters are weighted is visible to Admins only. You see each person's final score and tier.
- Every change is listed in **History** at the bottom of the person's page.
