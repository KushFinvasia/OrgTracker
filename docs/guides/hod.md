# Finvasia Elevate: guide for the HOD

You approve the Managers' check-ins, mark the people who report to you directly, and close the month.

## Each month

1. **Home** shows the department for the quarter: scores by month, teams by Manager, employee progress and who needs attention.
2. **Team → Waiting for my approval** lists submitted check-ins. Open a person to review, or tick several and use **Approve selected** / **Return selected** (a return needs a reason). Approving also verifies the training completions the Manager recorded.
3. **Validate delay reasons** on a person's page when a delay was not the person's fault. A validated delay does not count against them.
4. **Mark your own direct reports** (the Managers and analysts) the same way Managers do; an Admin approves those.
5. **Performance → Close month** once every check-in is approved. Closing freezes every result. You can close with check-ins still open only by giving a reason; those people are recorded as not evaluated.

## After closing

- **Rewards:** tiers (proposed: Gold 90, Silver 80, Bronze 70) and gates. Approve or reject **nominations**, then **Publish recognition**.
- **Corrections:** if a closed result is wrong, open the person's result in Performance and **Request correction** with a reason. An Admin approves; the Manager re-marks; you approve again. The old result is kept beside the new one.

## Good to know

- You cannot change a Manager's marks yourself; return the check-in with a reason instead.
- No one approves their own work: the person who marked a check-in cannot approve it.
- How scores are weighted is visible to Admins only.
