# Finvasia Elevate: guide for Admins

You see the whole pilot, own the scoring method, approve corrections and the check-ins the HOD writes.

## Regular tasks

- **Home:** organisation view for the quarter, department table, quarterly trend, teams by Manager.
- **Team → Waiting for my approval:** the HOD's direct reports (Managers and analysts). Approve or return like the HOD does.
- **Corrections:** a request from the HOD appears on the person's result page (Performance → person). **Approve** reopens only that person's month for their Manager; **Reject** needs a reason. You cannot approve a correction you requested yourself.
- **Name matches:** import warnings such as "Name match to confirm" can only be marked checked by an Admin, on the person's page in Team.

## KPI Scoring (Admin only)

- **Scoring method:** weights per category, curves, penalties, training (TWCS) credits and bands, coverage and tiers.
- **New version:** edit a copy, save it, check the **impact preview** for a month, then submit. **A second Admin approves**; open months from the effective month switch to it and closed months keep their rule.
- **KPI catalogue and import:** the KPI types and the Sales tracker import summary.
- **Demo data:** add or remove the labelled demo data (Jul–Oct 2026). Remove it before real use; real values and the audit history are never touched.

## Good to know

- Months open by themselves on the 1st (India time) and carry every person's KPIs and targets over from the previous month.
- Corrections, approvals, removals and every other change are in the append-only audit history.
